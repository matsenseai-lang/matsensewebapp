/ frequently asked

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. We scope engagements to fit your stage and budget, including a lighter model for pre-seed and seed teams shipping their first version.

Can you work under NDA?

Of course. Send us one, or we'll send you ours.

Do you offer retainers?

Yes. Many clients stay on a monthly retainer after launch, covering growth, maintenance and periodic redesign work — priced to fit your budget.

What if my budget is limited?

Tell us what you have to work with. We'll always be upfront about whether we can deliver within it, and we'd rather scope a smaller project properly than stretch it beyond what's realistic.

Do you offer AI consultancy separately from software development?

Yes. Our AI consultancy work — readiness audits, use-case scoping, and AI/ML product development — can be engaged on its own, or alongside a software project.
