      Questions

      Before you get in touch.

      What does a project cost?

      It depends on what you need. After a free call we give you a clear written quote before any work starts. If your budget is limited, tell us. We would rather scope a smaller project properly than stretch it too far.

      Do you work with small businesses and start-ups?

      Yes. Many of our clients are small, local businesses. We shape each project to fit your stage and budget.

      Is it safe to connect AI to our business data?

      We start with read-only access, keep the connector in your own UK cloud account and log every action. Anything that changes data or sends a message needs a person to approve it.

      Can you work under an NDA?

      Yes. Send us yours, or we will send you ours.

      Do you support the software after launch?

      Yes. Many clients stay on a monthly plan that covers updates, security and small improvements.
